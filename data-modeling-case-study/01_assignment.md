# Data Modeling Case Study Assignment
## Original Assignment Description

This assessment tests data modeling + SQL knowledge and how it helps answer main questions for a business team. The task involves analyzing the following diagram and answering business questions:
![Dimensional data model](images/diagram.png)

## Assignment Requirements

### Part One: Diagram Analysis
1. Describe the diagram.
2. What type of modeling is it?
3. What types of tables are shown?

### Part Two: Business Queries**
Based on the diagram, solve the following queries:
1. Tell me total Q4 2024 revenue.
2. Tell me Q4 2024 revenue by vertical.
3. Tell me Q4 2024 Year over Year growth by vertical.
4. Add in Payment method to the above query.

*Note: This assignment has been anonymized to remove any company-specific information while preserving the technical assessment structure.*
